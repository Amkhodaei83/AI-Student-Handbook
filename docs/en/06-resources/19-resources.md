## 🌟 Special Recommendation: Google's Sleeping Giant (Google AI Studio)

Before we review the list of tools, I have to introduce you to the biggest "cheat code" of 2026 that most students don't know about: **Google AI Studio**.

Most people use the public version of Gemini, which has many limitations. But Google has created a space for developers called [AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) that is **free** to use and gives you direct access to the masterpiece model, **Gemini 3 Pro (Preview)**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why is Gemini 3 Pro in AI Studio so unique?**
> This model has a **2-million-token context window**! What does that mean? It means you can upload an entire programming project's code, 50 PDF articles, and even 2-hour-long recorded videos of a professor's lecture all at once and say: *"Based on the course notes and this professor's video, tell me which topics I should focus on most for tomorrow's exam."*
> This model swallows and processes all the data in one go.

## 🇮🇷 Persian Open-Source Community & Tips (Persian Hubs)

To always stay one step ahead at university, you need to see what other smart students are doing. One of the best ways is to check out GitHub projects from Iranians.

**Project: Bypassing the Quera Plagiarism Detection System:**
The Quera platform is used for university programming exercises and has a strict plagiarism detection system. In this educational repository, you'll learn how these anti-plagiarism systems work and how you can get AI-generated code past them (by understanding the detection mechanisms, not just by copy-pasting).
